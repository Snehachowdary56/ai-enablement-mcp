# Prompt engineering cheat sheet (one page)

## The 4-part prompt
1. **Role and goal:** "You are helping me reply to a customer about invoice due dates."
2. **Context:** paste the facts, or say "Use the company knowledge base."
3. **Task and format:** "Write a reply under 120 words with a bullet list of next steps."
4. **Checks:** "If you are not sure, say so. Quote the source for every policy."

## Do
- Give examples of a good answer.
- Ask for sources and check them.
- Break big jobs into steps: outline first, then each section.
- Say who the reader is (a customer, a manager, a new hire).

## Don't
- Paste passwords, API keys, customer personal information or unreleased financial results.
- Send AI output to a customer without reading and checking it.
- Ask for a number without asking where it came from.

## Good vs weak
| Weak | Better |
|---|---|
| "Summarize this." | "Summarize this contract for a sales manager in 5 bullets: price, term, renewal, termination, risks." |
| "What's our travel policy?" | "Using the company knowledge base, what can I claim for meals on a 2-day trip? Quote the source." |
