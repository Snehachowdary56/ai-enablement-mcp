# New Employee Onboarding

## First week
On day one you receive your laptop, badge and accounts. Your manager schedules a welcome meeting and assigns an onboarding buddy.
Complete the security awareness and privacy training in the learning portal during your first week.

## Benefits enrolment
Enrol in health and dental benefits within 30 days of your start date in the HR portal. Coverage starts on your first day.

## Hybrid work
Employees work in the office three days a week. Your team agrees which days with your manager.
