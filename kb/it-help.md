# IT Help Desk Guide

## Resetting your password
Go to the self-service password page and choose "Forgot password". You will get a code on your registered phone.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are changed every 180 days.

## VPN access
Install the company VPN client from the software centre and sign in with your work account and multi-factor authentication.
If the VPN fails to connect, restart the client, then open a ticket with the IT help desk.

## Opening a ticket
Open a ticket in the IT portal or email the help desk. Urgent issues (a system is down for many people) should be reported by phone.
