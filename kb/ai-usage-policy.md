# Responsible AI Usage Policy

## Approved AI tools
Use only company-approved AI assistants, which are connected to the company knowledge base through MCP. Do not paste customer data into public AI tools.

## What not to share
Never share passwords, API keys, customer personal information or unreleased financial results with an AI tool.

## Checking AI answers
AI answers can be wrong. Check facts, numbers and policy answers against the source document before you act on them or send them to a customer.

## Getting help
The AI Enablement team runs weekly drop-in sessions and workshops on prompt engineering and building n8n automations.
