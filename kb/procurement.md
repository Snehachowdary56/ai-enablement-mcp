# Purchasing and Vendor Requests

## Buying software
Request new software through the procurement form. IT security reviews every new vendor before purchase.
Software under $500 per year can be bought on a company card with manager approval.

## Purchase orders
Purchases of $5,000 or more need a purchase order and three quotes, unless the vendor is the only supplier.
