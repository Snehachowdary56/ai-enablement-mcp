# Expense Policy

## Meals while travelling
You can claim up to $75 CAD per day for meals on business travel. Alcohol is not reimbursed.
Keep itemized receipts for any meal over $25.

## Submitting a claim
Submit expense claims in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense. Attach a receipt for every item.
Your manager approves claims under $1,000; claims of $1,000 or more also need Finance approval.

## Mileage
Personal car use for business is reimbursed at $0.68 per kilometre. Commuting to your usual office is not reimbursed.
